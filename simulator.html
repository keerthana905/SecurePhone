Interactive Simulation Flow:
  1. Click & Hold Power Button: Hold (or click) the glowing side POWER button to open the Shutdown Confirmation screen.
  2. Confirm or Cancel: Choose Shutdown Simulation (Verify Identity) or click Cancel to abort and log the cancellation.
  3. Verify Identity (Success): Enter Demo PIN {{ selected_device.demo_sim_pin if selected_device else '2580' }} or click Demo Biometric (Pass) to watch the shutdown animation and reach the “Device Powered Off (Simulation)” screen.
  4. Failed Verification (Blocked): Enter a wrong PIN (e.g. 0000) or click Demo Biometric (Fail) to prevent shutdown, display an anti-theft error alert, and click Retry Verification.
  5. Restart Virtual Phone: From the powered-off screen, click Restart Virtual Phone & Try Again to boot back to the lock screen.
